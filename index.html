GRE Vocabulary Snapshot

A fast, bilingual way to lock in high-frequency GRE vocabulary. Built on all 995 words from the Manhattan Prep 1000 GRE Words list, with Chinese definitions and bilingual example sentences — made for students who read Chinese and need to get through their word list quickly (想要快速过一遍高频词汇的同学).

Start Review Quiz → View Mistake Book

What's inside

Three focused tools, nothing extra to slow you down.

🔁

Shuffled Review Quiz

Pick which word groups to study and a direction — English → recall Chinese, or Chinese → recall English. Words appear in random order. Press ← when you've recalled the answer to reveal it and self-grade (✓ / ✕); press → if you're stuck to see the full bilingual definition and example sentences — it's logged to your Mistake Book automatically.

📕

Mistake Book

Every word you miss or can't recall is saved automatically, along with a running mistake count. Sorted A–Z by default, or switch to sort by mistake count. Export the whole list as a PDF whenever you want a printable study sheet.

🧠

ADHD Reading Mode

Toggle Bionic Reading from the nav bar at any time. It bolds the first few letters of each English word to give your eyes an anchor point, which can make longer definitions and example sentences easier to scan and stay focused on.

Design

🌗

Light / Dark / Auto

Switch themes manually from the nav bar, or leave it on Auto (the default) to follow your system's light/dark setting automatically.

💻

Open source, no backend

A static HTML/CSS/JS site with no server required — all progress (theme, reading mode, mistake book) is saved locally in your browser.
